About Which Seating

Which Seating is an independent guide to seats at major US stadiums and arenas. Ticket maps show every section. We answer a smaller question: where to start, and what to skip, using facts we can cite.

What we cover

118 venues across the NFL, MLB, NBA, NHL, and major college football stadiums. A building shared by two teams has one page.

How we work

Every venue page starts from a public list and, when we could open one, the venue's own page. We write the reading method ourselves and we label it as a method. See sources and methods and the editorial policy.

Independence

Which Seating is not affiliated with any team, league, venue, or ticket seller. It is the stadium companion to Which Cabin.

See a mistake after a renovation? Write to us.

Which Seating tells you which seats to pick at major US stadiums and arenas, and which to skip. Facts come from public lists and official pages. Section numbers are printed only when a source prints them. Layouts change, so check the venue's current chart before you buy.
