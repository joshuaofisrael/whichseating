Privacy policy

Last updated 19 Nov 2026. This policy explains how Which Seating (whichseating.com), a trade name of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC, a Florida limited liability company, handles information.

Analytics

The public site measures visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics and Google Analytics 4 (GA4). The preview build loads neither tool.

Cloudflare Web Analytics is the beacon at static.cloudflareinsights.com. It counts page views. It does not use cookies or local storage and does not track you across sites. It records aggregated information such as the page visited, referrer, browser, and country.

Google Analytics 4 loads only after you accept the cookie banner. Until you choose, Consent Mode v2 defaults are denied for analytics storage, ad storage, ad user data, and ad personalization, and the Google tag is not requested. If you accept, the site loads gtag.js for the measurement ID in the build config, and Google may set analytics cookies. If you decline, the tag stays unloaded. The Cookie settings link in the footer opens that choice again. While the measurement ID is blank, accepting does not send data to Google.

Contact

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and we keep them only as long as needed. The address is on the contact page.

Advertising

Which Seating uses Google AdSense. Pages include the AdSense account meta tag and the AdSense script for publisher account ca-pub-5681559774142917. The site does not place ad units on pages yet, so you should not see display ads from this site today. When ad units go live, third party vendors including Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info/choices. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland), we will ask for consent through a certified consent management platform before any advertising cookies are set. Authorized sellers are listed in ads.txt. See also our advertising disclosure.

Cookies

Which Seating's own pages do not set cookies. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies. Google Analytics 4 may set cookies only after you accept. When ads are shown, Google and other vendors may set advertising cookies subject to the consent rules above. You can decline analytics, change that choice from Cookie settings, or block cookies in your browser. The guides work without them.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security and operations.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you via the contact page. Residents of some US states and other countries may have additional rights, which we will honour.

Children

The site is for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We will post any changes here with a new date.
