Privacy policy

Last updated 16 Nov 2026. This policy explains how Which Seating (whichseating.com), a trade name of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC, a Florida limited liability company, handles information.

Analytics

When the analytics beacon is enabled, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies or local storage and does not track you across sites. It records basic, aggregated information such as the page visited, referrer, browser type, and country. The preview of the site does not load that beacon.

Contact

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and we keep them only as long as needed. The address is on the contact page.

Advertising

The site shows no ads today. If we add ads in future, for example through Google AdSense, third party vendors including Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You will be able to opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info/choices. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland), we will ask for consent through a certified consent management platform before any advertising cookies are set. We will update this policy before any ads go live.

Cookies

Which Seating itself sets no cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works without them.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security and operations.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you via the contact page. Residents of some US states and other countries may have additional rights, which we will honour.

Children

The site is for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We will post any changes here with a new date.
