Which MLB ballparks have a retractable roof?

By Which Seating. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

The Wikipedia list of current MLB stadiums classifies all 29 ballparks by roof. 6 have a retractable roof, 1 is a fixed roof that does not open, and 22 are open air. Checking this before the shade question matters more in baseball than in most sports, because a day game can run three hours or longer under the sun.

Retractable roof (open or closed by game day): 6 ballparks

  • American Family Field, home to Milwaukee Brewers, in Milwaukee, Wisconsin.
  • Chase Field, home to Arizona Diamondbacks, in Phoenix, Arizona.
  • Daikin Park, home to Houston Astros, in Houston, Texas.
  • Globe Life Field, home to Texas Rangers, in Arlington, Texas.
  • LoanDepot Park, home to Miami Marlins, in Miami, Florida.
  • T-Mobile Park, home to Seattle Mariners, in Seattle, Washington.

Fixed roof (does not open): 1 ballpark

  • Tropicana Field, home to Tampa Bay Rays, in St. Petersburg, Florida.

Open air (no roof over the seats): 22 ballparks

  • Angel Stadium, home to Los Angeles Angels, in Anaheim, California.
  • Busch Stadium, home to St. Louis Cardinals, in St. Louis, Missouri.
  • Citi Field, home to New York Mets, in Queens, New York.
  • Citizens Bank Park, home to Philadelphia Phillies, in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.
  • Comerica Park, home to Detroit Tigers, in Detroit, Michigan.
  • Coors Field, home to Colorado Rockies, in Denver, Colorado.
  • Dodger Stadium, home to Los Angeles Dodgers, in Los Angeles, California.
  • Fenway Park, home to Boston Red Sox, in Boston, Massachusetts.
  • Great American Ball Park, home to Cincinnati Reds, in Cincinnati, Ohio.
  • Kauffman Stadium, home to Kansas City Royals, in Kansas City, Missouri.
  • Nationals Park, home to Washington Nationals, in Washington, D.C..
  • Oracle Park, home to San Francisco Giants, in San Francisco, California.
  • Oriole Park at Camden Yards, home to Baltimore Orioles, in Baltimore, Maryland.
  • Petco Park, home to San Diego Padres, in San Diego, California.
  • PNC Park, home to Pittsburgh Pirates, in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania.
  • Progressive Field, home to Cleveland Guardians, in Cleveland, Ohio.
  • Rate Field, home to Chicago White Sox, in Chicago, Illinois.
  • Sutter Health Park, home to Athletics, in West Sacramento, California.
  • Target Field, home to Minnesota Twins, in Minneapolis, Minnesota.
  • Truist Park, home to Atlanta Braves, in Cumberland, Georgia.
  • Wrigley Field, home to Chicago Cubs, in Chicago, Illinois.
  • Yankee Stadium, home to New York Yankees, in Bronx, New York.

The one fixed roof: Tropicana Field

Tropicana Field is the single MLB park on this list with a fixed, non opening roof, so sun is not a seating factor there the way it is at an open air park. A 2019 report cited on the same Wikipedia list said the team closed the ballpark's upper deck and reduced capacity; the Sections to skip guide carries that note and its source. Confirm the current chart before you buy.

Why roof class matters for your seat

At a retractable roof park, sun exposure depends on whether the roof is open for that game, which this list does not record game by game. At an open air park, sun and wind reach every seat, and which side of the field gets the afternoon sun depends on the park's orientation, a detail these sources do not publish.
