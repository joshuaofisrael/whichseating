Sections to skip

By Which Seating. Last verified 20 Nov 2026.

Skip a seat when the chart or the ticket says limited view, obstructed view, or partial view. Clubs rarely print a second list of bad section numbers. The words on that ticket are the list. A safety net, a closed deck, and a non baseball event are three other things a club sometimes prints, and they are not the same label.

Read the adjective the club used

Checked 20 Nov 2026. Three club pages show the vocabulary, and none of them hands you a permanent section blacklist.

  • The Brewers' ticket page for the 30 July 2025 game against the Cubs, still posted on the club site, says a limited number of tickets remained, "including Standing Room & Obstructed View seats." Obstructed View is a named bucket next to standing room. The page does not give section numbers. If you are buying a Brewers seat and the listing uses those words, you already have the warning. You do not need a blog to translate it.
  • The Dodgers' page for their 2022 World Series ticket drawing says the seats offered could have a partial, restricted, or obstructed view, and could be anywhere in the park. That page is about 2022, not about a 2026 section map. It is useful because it shows the three adjectives the club was willing to put in one sentence. If a current Dodgers listing uses one of them, believe the listing.
  • The Yankees' presale page for a Liverpool and Sporting CP soccer match at Yankee Stadium says the sightlines for that event may be obstructed from baseball seating locations. A section that faces the diamond can face a soccer setup badly. Check the event, not only the section number you liked for baseball.

A closed deck is not an obstructed seat

At Tropicana Field the question is whether the upper deck is on sale at all. The Wikipedia MLB list we used on 16 Nov 2026 gives a capacity of 25,000 and cites a 2019 USA Today report that the Rays closed the upper deck. Adam Berry's MLB.com Tropicana guide of 9 April 2026 lists capacity as 25,025 and says the upper deck has been closed since 2019. The Rays spent 2025 at George M. Steinbrenner Field and returned to the dome for 2026. Do not buy an upper deck seat on the theory that the return reopened it. The 2026 guide still says closed. The venue table on our Tropicana page still shows the older 25,000 figure from Wikipedia. Both numbers are cited. Neither one puts the upper deck back on the map.

Netting is a third category

The Red Sox netting page puts protective netting from Field Box Section 79 to Field Box Section 9, about 12 feet 8 inches above the field, and says fans there should still watch for bats and balls. It never says obstructed. A seat behind that net can be a close view of the field with a screen in front of it. Skip it only if you do not want the screen, or if the ticket adds a limited view line of its own. The Fenway page has the rest of the sightline notes.

Old removal plans go stale

On 24 October 2016, MLB.com's Bryan Hoch reported a Yankees announcement that obstructed view seats in the bleachers would be removed, about 1,100 of them, as part of a larger cut of just under 2,100 seats that also included about 600 Terrace Level seats. That article is here. It is a 2016 plan for the 2017 season. The premium seating page we opened on 20 Nov 2026 describes Legends, Champions, Delta SKY360, Ford Field MVP, and Jim Beam. It does not reprint a current bleacher obstructed list, and it does not confirm the 2016 removal in those words. Use the 2016 piece as history. Use the ticket in your hand for the game you are attending.

Fan sites still publish long pole lists. We do not copy them. If a venue page prints a numbered obstructed list, send it through contact and we will add it with the citation. Until then the ticket adjective, the closed deck, the netting note, and the event warning are the sourced checks.

Frequently asked questions

Which section numbers should I avoid?

The ones the venue or the ticket marks limited, obstructed, partial, or restricted. This guide does not keep a second blacklist. The Brewers page for 30 July 2025 names Obstructed View as inventory and does not list sections.

Is the Tropicana Field upper deck open in 2026?

No, according to the MLB.com Tropicana guide dated 9 April 2026, which says the upper deck has been closed since 2019 and lists capacity as 25,025. The Rays played 2025 at George M. Steinbrenner Field and returned to Tropicana Field for 2026.

Does Fenway netting mean the seat is obstructed?

The Red Sox netting page does not use that word. The screen runs from Field Box Section 79 to Field Box Section 9. An obstructed label on the ticket would be an additional fact.

Can a good baseball seat be a bad seat at another event?

Yes. The Yankees' page for a soccer match at Yankee Stadium says sightlines from baseball seating locations may be obstructed for that event.
