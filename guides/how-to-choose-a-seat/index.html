How to choose a seat

By Which Seating. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

Read the venue page, then the official page it links, then the ticket. Use the lowest level near the middle of the playing surface for a straight on view. Skip any seat marked limited view. Do not trust a section number that Which Seating did not copy from a source.

Steps

  1. Open the venue in this guide. Start with a league: NFL, MLB, NBA, NHL, or college football.
  2. Read the roof line. A dome, a fixed roof, or an indoor arena changes the sun question. A retractable roof depends on the event. Shade and sun.
  3. Use the official page linked on the venue for the real section map. Our bowl drawing is generic.
  4. On the ticket, reject limited view, obstructed view, and partial view unless you mean to buy that seat. Sections to skip.
  5. For step free seats and companion seats, use the venue accessibility office. Accessible seating.

Examples

  • Lambeau Field, an open air NFL stadium.
  • AT&T Stadium, a retractable NFL roof.
  • Caesars Superdome, a dome.
  • Yankee Stadium, a baseball park with an official MLB.com ballpark page.
  • Madison Square Garden, an indoor arena shared by two teams.

Frequently asked questions

What is the safest way to pick a section?

Use the venue's own chart and the words on the ticket. Which Seating will not give you a section number the venue did not publish in our sources.

Do you rank sections by price?

No. Prices change by opponent and by day. We do not publish a value ranking.
