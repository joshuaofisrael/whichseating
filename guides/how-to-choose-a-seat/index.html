How to choose a seat

By Which Seating. Last verified 20 Nov 2026.

Open the venue page, then the official page it links, then the ticket. Use a low seat near the middle of the playing surface when the club has not described the view in its own words. When it has, use those words. Skip any seat marked limited view. Ignore a section number that Which Seating did not copy from a source.

Do this in order

  1. Open the venue. Start from a league if you are not sure of the building: NFL, MLB, NBA, NHL, or college football.
  2. Read the roof line before you ask about sun. A dome, a fixed roof, or an indoor arena takes weather off the table. A retractable roof depends on that event. Shade and sun is the longer version, including the Fenway case where an open park still has a roof over the infield grandstand.
  3. Look for the club's own view language. On 20 Nov 2026 the Yankees premium seating page uses three different adjectives: intimate for the Legends Suite between sections 014A and 027A, expansive for the Delta SKY360 Suite on the Main Level, and sweeping for the Jim Beam Club on the Terrace Level. Those are not synonyms. Where to sit to see the whole field lines them up.
  4. Use the official section map for numbers. Our bowl drawing is generic. At Wrigley the Cubs' own FAQ is the key: since 2019, L on an upper section means left field and R means right field.
  5. On the ticket, reject limited view, obstructed view, and partial view unless you mean to sit there. A safety net is not the same label. Fenway's net runs from Field Box Section 79 to Field Box Section 9 and the Red Sox page does not call it obstructed. Sections to skip.
  6. For a step free seat or a companion seat, use the venue accessibility office. Accessible seating.
  7. If the ticket names a club, read the amenity list before you treat the club as a better view. What club seats include.

One worked baseball pass

Say you are choosing among three parks and you care about seeing the field, not about a lounge.

  • Yankee Stadium. The Yankees put the most intimate view of the diamond on the Legends Suite, between sections 014A and 027A, and a sweeping view on the Terrace. The Champions Suite, just past first and third, is described with food and a lounge, not with a view adjective. Read the product name.
  • Fenway Park. MLB.com's 18 March 2026 guide calls the lower boxes a close view and the infield grandstand a strong sightline under a roof. Monster Seats are above the 37 foot wall. The net in front of Field Box 79 through Field Box 9 is published as protection.
  • Wrigley Field. The Cubs say their old 400 level, now the 300s, compares with 200 level seats at other parks. The letter after the section number points you to a ramp. It does not grade the seat.

Other buildings in this guide still have only the chart method, because their pages did not print a view sentence we could quote on 20 Nov 2026. Examples already on the site: Lambeau Field is open air, AT&T Stadium has a retractable roof, Caesars Superdome is a dome, and Madison Square Garden is an indoor arena used by two teams.

Frequently asked questions

What is the safest way to pick a section?

Use the venue's own chart and the words on the ticket. If the club has described the view, use that sentence. Which Seating will not give you a section number the venue did not publish in our sources.

Is the lowest seat always the best view of the field?

It is the fallback when the club is silent. The Yankees call a Field Level suite intimate and a Terrace club sweeping. The Cubs compare an upper level with 200 level seats elsewhere. Read the page for that park.

Do you rank sections by price?

No. Prices change by opponent and by day. We do not publish a value ranking.
