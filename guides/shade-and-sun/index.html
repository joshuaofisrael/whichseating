Shade and sun

By Which Seating. Last verified 20 Nov 2026.

Sun depends on the roof class in the source list. We do not publish a shade map by section, because the lists we use do not print one.

Roof classes in this guide

  • Open air. Sun and weather reach the seats. Example: Lambeau Field. We still do not name a sunny section.
  • Retractable. Sun depends on whether the roof is open that day. Examples: AT&T Stadium and Chase Field.
  • Dome. The roof stays closed in normal use, so sun is not a seating factor. Example: Ford Field.
  • Skylight. A fixed roof, not open stands. The list does not say which sections get more light. Example: SoFi Stadium.
  • Fixed. A baseball roof that does not open. Example: Tropicana Field.
  • Indoor arena. Sun is not the way to pick a seat. Example: Madison Square Garden.
  • Not stated. The college list we used has no roof column. Example: Michigan Stadium. We do not guess.

When "open" still has a roof over some seats

Checked 20 Nov 2026. The stadium list is still the roof class. Two ballpark guides add a sentence the list does not.

Fenway Park stays Open on the list. Ian Browne's MLB.com Fenway guide of 18 March 2026 says the wooden grandstand seats in the infield have a roof overhead that keeps rain off the people in that band. That is cover for one slice of an open park. It is not a shade chart, and it does not say which baseline gets the afternoon sun. The lower boxes and the bleachers are outside that sentence. Details and the Red Sox dimension list are on the Fenway page.

Tropicana Field is the fixed roof on the MLB list, the one that does not open. Adam Berry's MLB.com Tropicana guide of 9 April 2026 still treats it as a dome and lists capacity as 25,025, with the upper deck closed since 2019. The same guide says the Rays played the 2025 season at George M. Steinbrenner Field in Tampa and came back to Tropicana Field for 2026 after the repairs. Sun is still not a seating factor under that fixed roof. The upper deck is still the section group to avoid assuming is on sale. Our venue table still shows 25,000 from the Wikipedia list retrieved 16 Nov 2026. The newer guide figure is 25,025. We are not papering over the difference.

Neither page names a sunny section number. If a club has not printed one, this site will not invent one. How to choose a seat puts the roof step before the view step.

Frequently asked questions

Do you publish a sunny section for each park?

No. A shade map by section was not in the sources, so naming one would be a guess.

What if the roof can open?

Treat a retractable roof as open air when it is open, and as a closed building when it is shut. The list does not say which sections are shaded either way.

Does open air mean every seat is in the sun?

No. Fenway Park is Open on the stadium list. MLB.com's Fenway guide dated 18 March 2026 says the wooden infield grandstand has a roof overhead that keeps rain off those seats. That is one covered band, not a shade map of the park.

Is Tropicana Field still a fixed roof with the upper deck closed?

The MLB.com Tropicana guide dated 9 April 2026 still describes the dome and says the upper deck has been closed since 2019. It lists capacity as 25,025. The Rays played 2025 at George M. Steinbrenner Field and returned to Tropicana Field for 2026.
