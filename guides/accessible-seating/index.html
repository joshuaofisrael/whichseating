Accessible seating

By Which Seating. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

Accessible section numbers were not in the text sources we checked, so this guide does not guess them. The venue accessibility office and the accessible path on the official page are the source.

What to ask the venue

  • Which sections have wheelchair spaces and companion seats for this event.
  • Whether the seat is step free from the gate you will use.
  • How to buy that seat. Standard maps often hide accessible inventory until you ask.

Official pages we did open, and where you should start, include Lambeau Field, Madison Square Garden, and Yankee Stadium. None of those pages gave us a plain text list of accessible section numbers on the check dated 16 Nov 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Which section is accessible?

We do not name one. Ask the venue. Accessible inventory is not the same as the standard section map.

Can a companion sit with me?

Venues sell companion seats next to many wheelchair spaces. The venue, not this page, confirms the pair for your event.
