Accessible seating: what venues publish

By Which Seating. Last verified 19 Nov 2026.

Accessible seats are a separate inventory from the standard section map. Buy the wheelchair or companion ticket the venue sells for that event, and confirm the path from your gate. This guide cites only section lists that the venue itself publishes in plain text.

What every fan should ask before buying

  • Which ticket type you need: wheelchair space, companion seat, aisle transfer seat, or semi ambulatory seat.
  • Whether the path from the gate on your ticket is step free to that section.
  • How many companion seats you can buy next to the accessible space.
  • Whether the venue relocates season tickets game by game when accessible inventory is still open.

What official ADA pages publish today

These facts come from the venue pages linked in Sources, checked 19 Nov 2026. Layouts change, so re check the live page before you buy.

Lambeau Field

The Packers guests with disabilities page says you must obtain wheelchair and companion tickets before entering if you will watch from a wheelchair or scooter. Season ticket holders can trade non accessible tickets for wheelchair and companion seats game by game when inventory remains, with a stated limit of one wheelchair and one companion seat per swap. Wheelchair and companion season seats are sold to people whose disability requires that seating, with a stated limit of four tickets (one wheelchair and up to three companions). The page does not print a public list of accessible section numbers.

Yankee Stadium

The Yankees Guide for Guests with Disabilities says wheelchair accessible and designated aisle transfer seats are sold at various price points and locations. The same page lists electrical outlets for recharging mobility equipment at wheelchair accessible seating areas in sections 021B, 029, 104 through 106, 109, 111, 114A, 116, 118 through 121B, 124, 127A, 130, 132 through 134, 211, 225, 226, 305, 306, 309, 310, 313 through 315, 317 through 320A, 320C through 323, 325 through 327, 330, 331, 333 and 334, plus bleacher sections 203 and 235 through 237. It also states there are 16 public elevators. For current seat locations, the page directs guests to Disabled Services rather than a printable chart of every open wheelchair row.

Angel Stadium

The Angels accessible seating page says more than 500 wheelchair, semi ambulatory, and companion tickets are available. It lists accessible seating on the Terrace Level in sections 201 through 213 and 221 through 233, the Left Field Pavilion in sections 257 through 260, and the Right Field Pavilion in sections 241 through 249. It also lists accessible seats in premium club areas including the Lexus Diamond Club (sections 114 through 122) and Club Level sections 322 and 330, and notes that seating in those premium areas offers in seat food and beverage service. A buyer of an accessible seat may purchase up to three additional contiguous companion seats in the same row when they remain available.

LoanDepot Park

The Marlins disability access guide says wheelchair accessible and semi ambulatory seats with operable armrests sit on all ballpark levels, and one companion or escort may accompany a guest with a disability in the designated areas. The same guide lists electrical outlets at wheelchair accessible seating on the Promenade Level (sections 1, 9 through 20, 28, 30 through 31, 35 and 39), Legends Level (sections 205, 211, 220 and 223), and Vista Level (sections 309 through 311, 317, 319, 320 and 322). Wheelchair transport is offered from an entrance to a seating section and back.

Madison Square Garden

The Madison Square Garden site states that the arena provides wheelchair seats, designated aisle transfer seats, and companion seats, plus accessible restrooms and assisted listening devices. It does not publish a plain text list of accessible section numbers on the pages we opened; it points guests to Accessibility Services for event specific inventory.

Frequently asked questions

Which section is accessible?

Only sections the venue lists for accessible inventory. Yankee Stadium, Angel Stadium, and LoanDepot Park publish section lists on their ADA pages linked in Sources. Lambeau Field and Madison Square Garden direct you to their accessibility offices instead of a public section list.

Can a companion sit with me?

Yes when the venue sells companion seats. The Angels page allows up to three contiguous companions with an accessible seat when available. The Marlins guide allows one companion or escort in designated areas. Lambeau Field states limits on wheelchair and companion pairs. Confirm the pair on the ticket for your event.

Do accessible seats appear on the normal map?

Often not until you ask for accessible inventory. Treat the ADA page and the ticket office path as the source, not a generic seating diagram.

What if my ticketed seat does not work for my disability?

Several venues describe a relocation process through guest services when a ticketed seat cannot accommodate a disability. Ask at the venue; this guide does not invent a relocation rule.
