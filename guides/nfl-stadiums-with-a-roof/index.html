Which NFL stadiums have a roof?

By Which Seating. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

The Wikipedia list of current NFL stadiums classifies all 30 stadiums by roof. 20 are open air, 5 have a retractable roof, 3 have a fixed skylight roof, and 2 are a dome. The roof class is the first fact worth checking before you weigh sun, wind, or rain for a section.

Dome (closed in normal use): 2 stadiums

  • Caesars Superdome, home to New Orleans Saints, in New Orleans, Louisiana.
  • Ford Field, home to Detroit Lions, in Detroit, Michigan.

Retractable roof (open or closed by game day): 5 stadiums

  • AT&T Stadium, home to Dallas Cowboys, in Arlington, Texas.
  • Lucas Oil Stadium, home to Indianapolis Colts, in Indianapolis, Indiana.
  • Mercedes-Benz Stadium, home to Atlanta Falcons, in Atlanta, Georgia.
  • Reliant Stadium, home to Houston Texans, in Houston, Texas.
  • State Farm Stadium, home to Arizona Cardinals, in Glendale, Arizona.

Skylight roof (a fixed roof, not open stands): 3 stadiums

  • Allegiant Stadium, home to Las Vegas Raiders, in Paradise, Nevada.
  • SoFi Stadium, home to Los Angeles Rams and Los Angeles Chargers, in Inglewood, California.
  • U.S. Bank Stadium, home to Minnesota Vikings, in Minneapolis, Minnesota.

Open air (no roof over the seats): 20 stadiums

  • Acrisure Stadium, home to Pittsburgh Steelers, in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania.
  • Arrowhead Stadium, home to Kansas City Chiefs, in Kansas City, Missouri.
  • Bank of America Stadium, home to Carolina Panthers, in Charlotte, North Carolina.
  • Empower Field at Mile High, home to Denver Broncos, in Denver, Colorado.
  • EverBank Stadium, home to Jacksonville Jaguars, in Jacksonville, Florida.
  • Gillette Stadium, home to New England Patriots, in Foxborough, Massachusetts.
  • Hard Rock Stadium, home to Miami Dolphins, in Miami Gardens, Florida.
  • Highmark Stadium, home to Buffalo Bills, in Orchard Park, New York.
  • Huntington Bank Field, home to Cleveland Browns, in Cleveland, Ohio.
  • Lambeau Field, home to Green Bay Packers, in Green Bay, Wisconsin.
  • Levi's Stadium, home to San Francisco 49ers, in Santa Clara, California.
  • Lincoln Financial Field, home to Philadelphia Eagles, in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.
  • Lumen Field, home to Seattle Seahawks, in Seattle, Washington.
  • M&T Bank Stadium, home to Baltimore Ravens, in Baltimore, Maryland.
  • MetLife Stadium, home to New York Giants and New York Jets, in East Rutherford, New Jersey.
  • Nissan Stadium, home to Tennessee Titans, in Nashville, Tennessee.
  • Northwest Stadium, home to Washington Commanders, in Landover, Maryland.
  • Paycor Stadium, home to Cincinnati Bengals, in Cincinnati, Ohio.
  • Raymond James Stadium, home to Tampa Bay Buccaneers, in Tampa, Florida.
  • Soldier Field, home to Chicago Bears, in Chicago, Illinois.

Why the roof class matters for your seat

A dome, or a retractable roof that stays closed for the game, removes sun and weather from the seating decision. A skylight roof still lets in daylight through a fixed structure, but it is not open to the sky the way a true open air bowl is. At an open air stadium, sun and wind are back on the table, and which side of the field gets the afternoon sun depends on the building's orientation, a detail this stadium list does not publish. Frequently asked questions

Which NFL stadiums have a dome?

On the current Wikipedia stadium list: Caesars Superdome, Ford Field.

Which NFL stadiums have a retractable roof?

On the current Wikipedia stadium list: AT&T Stadium, Lucas Oil Stadium, Mercedes-Benz Stadium, Reliant Stadium, State Farm Stadium.

Which NFL stadiums are open air with no roof?

20 of the 30 stadiums on the list have no roof: Acrisure Stadium, Arrowhead Stadium, Bank of America Stadium, Empower Field at Mile High, EverBank Stadium, Gillette Stadium, Hard Rock Stadium, Highmark Stadium, Huntington Bank Field, Lambeau Field, Levi's Stadium, Lincoln Financial Field, Lumen Field, M&T Bank Stadium, MetLife Stadium, Nissan Stadium, Northwest Stadium, Paycor Stadium, Raymond James Stadium, Soldier Field.

Does a retractable roof mean the stadium is always closed?

No. A retractable roof can be open or closed for a given game. The source list does not say how often each team opens it, so this page does not guess.
