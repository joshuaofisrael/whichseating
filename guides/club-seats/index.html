What do NFL and MLB club seats include?

By Which Seating. Last verified 19 Nov 2026.

Club seats are a premium ticket tier. What you get is whatever that venue prints for that club: lounge access, padded seats, private entrances, and sometimes included food or in seat service. This page compares only official club pages we opened on 19 Nov 2026. It does not publish prices, and it does not invent a package for a stadium we did not check.

What "club seats" usually means on official pages

Across the NFL and MLB club pages below, three promises show up again and again:

  • Lounge access. A climate controlled or private club space behind or beside the seats, often open before kickoff or first pitch and for a stated window after the game.
  • Better seat hardware. Wider or padded seats, sometimes with extra leg room, in a named club section.
  • Separate entry. VIP or private entrances that skip the general gate line.

Food and drink are not one rule. Some clubs include food and selected drinks. Others sell specialty food inside the lounge. Always read the amenity list for the exact club name on your ticket.

NFL examples from team club pages

Lucas Oil Stadium (Indianapolis Colts)

The Colts club seats page describes the KennMar East Club and Ascension St. Vincent West Club. It lists padded seats with extra leg room between the 25 yard lines, VIP entrances for premium ticket holders, club lounges with live music and complimentary samplings, and a concierge desk in each lounge. Lounge sampling is described as available from gate open until kickoff while supplies last. The page does not claim a full all inclusive meal plan for every club seat.

Empower Field at Mile High (Denver Broncos)

The Broncos United Globe Club page lists club level access three hours before kickoff and up to one hour after the game, exclusive premium entrances, extra wide 22 inch padded seats, and high end food and beverage with named action stations, pizza ovens, and full service bars. West Club sections 300 through 315 and East Club sections 331 through 346 are named on that page.

Soldier Field (Chicago Bears)

The Bears United Club page places club seats on the east side of Soldier Field on the 200 and 300 levels and lists climate controlled indoor lounge access for club seat ticket holders. It says the lounge opens two hours before kickoff and stays open about five hours after the game starts. Amenities named on the page include private entrances and restrooms, specialty food, 12 full service bars, 21 inch wide cushioned seats, a private team store, and more than 140 indoor TVs. The page also lists the United Club section numbers on those two levels.

MLB examples from team premium pages

LoanDepot Park (Miami Marlins)

The Marlins PNC Club page lists valet parking, a private entrance, all inclusive food and beverage including house wine, beer, and soft drinks, a private lounge, large cushioned seats, and in seat service. It states the club is available to Marlins Members.

Angel Stadium (Los Angeles Angels)

The Angels Don Julio Club page places seats behind home plate on the Terrace Level, with executive style seating and countertops, exclusive lounge access, food and non alcoholic beverages included, and preferred parking options. It does not describe in seat delivery on that page.

Are club seats "worth it"?

Which Seating does not publish prices or a value score. Worth depends on which printed amenity you actually need:

  1. Watching the play. A club seat still has to face the field well. Use How to choose a seat and the venue chart. A lounge does not fix a bad angle.
  2. Weather. An indoor lounge helps at an open air stadium when you can leave your seat. It does not change the roof over the seat itself. Check Which NFL stadiums have a roof and Which MLB ballparks have a retractable roof first.
  3. Food included vs for purchase. The Marlins PNC Club and Angels Don Julio Club pages describe included food. The Colts club page emphasizes lounge samplings and specialty menus. The Broncos page highlights action stations and bars. Match the ticket name to the amenity list.
  4. In seat service. The Marlins PNC Club page names in seat service. The Angels accessible seating page notes in seat service in some premium accessible areas. Do not assume every club seat includes runners.
  5. Access time. Broncos and Bears pages state lounge open windows before and after the game. If early entry matters, verify the hours on the club page for that season.

If the official page does not list lounge access, covered seating, or in seat service for the product on your ticket, this site will not invent them. Open the venue page on Which Seating, then the official premium link in its sources.

Frequently asked questions

What do club seats include?

Whatever that venue lists for the named club: often lounge access, padded seats, and a private entrance. Food may be included or sold separately. Read the club page for the ticket name you hold.

Do club seats come with in seat service?

Only when the venue says so. The Marlins PNC Club page names in seat service. Other club pages we checked emphasize lounges and bars instead. Do not assume runners at every club seat.

Are club seats covered from sun and rain?

Not automatically. A climate controlled lounge is not the same as a roof over your seat. Check the stadium roof class and the club page before you buy.

Are club seats worth the upgrade?

This site does not publish prices or a value score. Decide from the printed amenities: lounge hours, included food, seat width, private entry, and whether the view matches what you want to watch.
