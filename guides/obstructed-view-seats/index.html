How do you spot an obstructed view seat before you buy?

By Which Seating. Last verified 20 Nov 2026.

Spot an obstructed view seat by the word on the club's own listing. Obstructed, limited, partial, and restricted are the labels. A safety net and a closed deck are different facts, and a fan site pole chart is not a source. Checked 20 Nov 2026.

Do this before you pay

  1. Read the listing for obstructed, limited, partial, or restricted. If one of those words is there, you already have the club's warning. Sections to skip is the longer writeup of the same check.
  2. Do not fill in a section number the club left blank. The Cubs renumbering FAQ explains L and R and the 2019 number change. It does not name an obstructed row. The Wrigley page stops at what that FAQ prints.
  3. Separate the net from the label. At Fenway the net covers a published span of Field Box seats and the Red Sox page still does not call them obstructed. Skip the net only if you do not want a screen.
  4. Separate a closed deck from a bad view. At Tropicana Field the 9 April 2026 guide still says the upper deck is closed. Do not buy it on the theory that the 2026 return reopened it.
  5. Do not treat a 2016 renovation note as this year's map. On 24 October 2016 Bryan Hoch reported for MLB.com that the Yankees planned to remove obstructed view bleacher seats, about 1,100 of them, inside a cut of just under 2,100 seats. The premium seating page opened on 20 Nov 2026 describes current clubs and does not reprint that bleacher list. History is not the ticket in your hand.

If a venue later prints a numbered obstructed list, that list belongs on the venue page with a citation. Until then the listing word, the netting note, the closed deck, and the event warning are the checks. How to choose a seat puts this step after the roof and the chart. Frequently asked questions

How do I know a seat is obstructed before I buy?

Read the club's listing for obstructed, limited, partial, or restricted. The Brewers' page for 30 July 2025 names Obstructed View as inventory and does not list sections. The word is the warning.

Is Fenway netting an obstructed view?

No. The Red Sox netting page puts the screen from Field Box Section 79 to Field Box Section 9 and does not use the word obstructed.

Did the Yankees remove obstructed bleacher seats?

A 24 October 2016 MLB.com report said the club planned to remove about 1,100 obstructed view bleacher seats. The premium seating page checked on 20 Nov 2026 does not confirm a current list. Use the ticket for your event.

Is the Tropicana upper deck the same as an obstructed seat?

No. The MLB.com guide dated 9 April 2026 says the upper deck has been closed since 2019. That is a deck off sale, not a pole in front of an open seat.
