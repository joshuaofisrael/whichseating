Image policy

Last updated 19 Nov 2026.

Diagrams

The bowl drawing on a venue page is an original diagram. It is not a copy of a seating chart, a broadcast graphic, or a team map. The caption on that drawing links to this page.

Photos

Guide articles may use one realistic news style thumbnail. The caption says AI-generated image and links to this policy. Those images do not depict a real person, a readable logo, or a copied seat chart. The site logo and the default social card are simple seat drawings we made for the site.

AI-generated images

AI-generated illustrations are labelled in the caption as "AI-generated image" and link here. They are not photographs of a specific branded stadium event.

Questions

If you believe an image uses your work, see our copyright policy or contact us.

Which Seating tells you which seats to pick at major US stadiums and arenas, and which to skip. Facts come from public lists and official pages. Section numbers are printed only when a source prints them. Layouts change, so check the venue's current chart before you buy.
