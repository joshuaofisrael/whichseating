Michigan is the stadium that prints a row key

The Michigan athletics 2026 preferred seat map organizes Michigan Stadium into three row bands. Lower is rows A through 30. Middle is rows 31 through 60. Upper is row 61 and above. The same page names the horizontal zones the school uses, including Victors, Valiant, Maize, Blue, and End Zone, and it shows suites on the east and west sides. Contribution amounts are on that map. This page does not repeat them. A price is not a view.

End zone is its own zone on that map, not a nickname for "bad seats." The lowest end zone seats are close to one goal line and far from the other. The lowest sideline seats are the straight on view of the whole field. That tradeoff is the football method on how to choose a seat. Michigan's map is the school saying the bowl is cut both ways: height in three bands, and location in named zones.

Students are a published block, not a rumor

The 2026 Michigan student football ticket policy says the student section begins at the aisle break in section 25 and extends through section 34 if needed. Seats inside that block are assigned by priority points from the prior season. The highest totals sit in the lowest rows. Incoming students, who have no points yet, sit in the upper rows. Points reset each season. The policy says incoming students cannot be seated with returning students. That is one school's student block. It is not the student section at Ohio Stadium, Beaver Stadium, or Neyland Stadium.

Frequently asked questions

Which college football seats are in the shade?

This page does not name them. On 21 Nov 2026 the FBS stadium list still had no roof column. The columns are stadium, city, state, team, conference, capacity, record, built, expanded, and surface.

What do lower, middle, and upper mean at Michigan Stadium?

On Michigan's 2026 preferred seat map, lower is rows A through 30, middle is rows 31 through 60, and upper is row 61 and above. End zone is a named zone on the same map.

Where do Michigan students sit?

The 2026 student ticket policy says the student section starts at the aisle break in section 25 and can run through section 34. Higher priority points sit in lower rows. Incoming students start in the upper rows.

Can I use the Michigan map at another stadium?

No. Ohio Stadium, Beaver Stadium, Neyland, and the Rose Bowl are different buildings. Open that stadium's page. A Michigan row number is not theirs.
