Which seats face the floor at an NBA arena?

By Which Seating. Last verified 21 Nov 2026.

There are 29 NBA venues in this guide. A concert in the same building often uses a different chart from the game. The notes under this paragraph were checked 21 Nov 2026.

This photo is a generic bowl at dusk. It is not an NBA arena, and it is not a seating chart.

A game chart and a concert chart are not the same file

All 29 buildings on this list are indoor, so sun is not how you pick an NBA seat. The basketball method is still the old one: the lowest seats at midcourt are the closest straight on view of the whole floor, and the lowest seats behind a basket look at the near hoop. That is a way to read a game chart. It is a bad way to read a concert, because several of these arenas publish a different document for a show. Checked 21 Nov 2026.

When the arena's own pages disagree on capacity

Crypto.com Arena is the caution against copying one number. On 21 Nov 2026 the A to Z guide says basketball capacity is 19,079, hockey is 18,118, and concerts and other special events near 20,000 depending on the setup. The vital stats page says 18,997 for the Lakers, 18,145 for the Kings, and 19,395 for concerts, and that two thirds of the seating is in the lower level. The Wikipedia NBA arena list figure on our venue page is 18,997 for basketball, which matches vital stats and not the A to Z guide. The NHL list figure we carry for the Kings is 18,230, which matches neither official page. The Crypto.com Arena page keeps the list figures in the table. This paragraph is here so a concert capacity and a game capacity are not treated as one fact.

The same A to Z guide is useful for the floor question even without a cleaned up capacity. It says a GA floor ticket is standing room only, with no seats, entered as "GA FLR" at the Kobe Bryant entrance, and that some shows use a different setup. Levels it names: event level for floor and GA tickets, main concourse for 100 and 200 level tickets, three suite levels, and an upper concourse for 300 level tickets. That stack is this building's stack. It is not the United Center's 100, 200, and 300, and it is not Barclays' upper pavilion.

Check a building, not the league average

The league page cannot tell you the row. These four publish the concert difference in their own words.

  • T-Mobile Arena
  • Prudential Center
  • United Center
  • Barclays Center

Madison Square Garden's concert seat map, opened the same day, says stage setup and seat configuration varies by event. The Garden page is the one to open for Knicks and Rangers. A shared building can sit on this list and on the NHL list. Use the team on the ticket, then the chart with that event's name.

Frequently asked questions

Which NBA seats face the whole floor?

On a game chart, the lowest seats at midcourt are the closest straight on view of the whole floor. The lowest seats behind a basket look at the near hoop. A concert in the same building can use a different chart.

Do NBA arenas publish a separate concert map?

Several do. T-Mobile Arena prints an end stage capacity and a center stage capacity. Prudential Center publishes an end stage map and a center stage map. United Center publishes a reserved floor concert chart and a general admission floor concert chart. Barclays Center publishes a concerts chart and says a GA floor ticket is not a seat.

Why do some capacity numbers disagree?

Crypto.com Arena's A to Z guide says basketball capacity is 19,079 and concerts near 20,000. Its vital stats page says 18,997 for the Lakers and 19,395 for concerts. The Wikipedia figure on our venue table matches 18,997. We keep both official numbers.

Does the sun matter at an NBA arena?

No. Every arena on this list is indoor, so sun is not the seating factor.
