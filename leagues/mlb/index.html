How do I choose seats for MLB?

On a baseball seating chart, seats in the lowest level directly behind home plate face the pitcher and the full field. Seats down the first base line or the third base line look across the diamond. Upper levels are farther from home plate. Outfield seats face back toward the infield. Then open the venue page and the official page linked there.
