Which seats are best at T-Mobile Arena?

By Which Seating. Last verified 21 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • End stage. Both official pages print 12,000 to 18,000 for an end stage concert.
  • Center stage. Both print 19,500 to 20,000. They do not list which sections make up the gap.
  • Loge. The A to Z guide places loge boxes at one end of the lower level, opposite the stage, for concerts. Club seats are the two center sections. Neither sentence numbers the sections.
  • GA floor. Standing room only, no seats, wristband. Basketball capacity is 18,000 on the info page and 19,000 on the A to Z guide.

T-Mobile Arena in Paradise, Nevada is listed as home to Vegas Golden Knights. Hockey capacity 17,367. Roof on the source list: Indoor.

How to read the chart

Start with the official page linked in the sources. On a hockey seating chart, the lowest seats at center ice are the closest straight on view of the whole rink. The lowest seats behind a goal look at the near net from the end. Upper levels are higher. This is our method for reading a chart. It is not a list of section numbers from the venue.

Seven levels, and a loge aimed at the far end of the stage

The A to Z guide lists seven levels and then warns that not all of them open for every event: event level on the ground, mezzanine, main concourse, City National Bank suite level, Allegiant Flight Deck, upper concourse, and the tower level with Hyde Lounge on the 7th level.

The loge boxes are the closest the guide comes to a concert view sentence. It says they fit four to six guests and "will be perfectly situated in one end of the lower level at the opposite end of the stage for viewing concerts." That is the arena describing its loge product: the end of the lower bowl facing the stage, not a side seat and not the floor. The same paragraph adds drink service, a TV for every two seats, and a private lounge. It does not say every lower level seat has that view.

Club seat memberships are a different product. The guide says they are a limited number of in bowl seats in the two center sections, with access to Ghost Lounge and the Bud Light Lounge. "Center sections" is the arena's phrase. It does not print the section numbers, and it does not say those two sections face an end stage the way the loge does.

The floor ticket with no chair

General admission on the floor is standing room only. The A to Z guide says there are no seats. After you enter you get a wristband and you are directed to aisles that reach the floor. People who want to line up early are asked to start no earlier than 4 p.m. Doors for concerts generally open 60 minutes before the event, and the guide says that can vary. Seats that do exist run about 18 to 22 inches wide.

The arena info page describes the building as being in Las Vegas. The NHL list we used files the city as Paradise. The page you are on keeps Paradise because that is the list's place name. The arena's own wording is Las Vegas. Home team on the list: Vegas Golden Knights. The NHL hub is the parent. How to choose a seat still applies after you know which capacity you bought: end stage, center stage, or a floor with no seat.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best seats at T-Mobile Arena?

The arena does not rank sections. It prints 12,000 to 18,000 for an end stage concert and 19,500 to 20,000 for a center stage concert, and it says loge boxes sit at the end of the lower level opposite the stage.

How many people fit at a T-Mobile Arena concert?

End stage concerts are listed at 12,000 to 18,000. Center stage concerts are listed at 19,500 to 20,000. The info page and the A to Z guide agree on those two ranges.

Is the T-Mobile Arena floor a seat?

Not on a GA floor ticket. The A to Z guide says the floor is standing room only, with no seats, and that you receive a wristband inside.

Why do the basketball numbers disagree?

The arena info page says 18,000 for basketball. The A to Z guide says 19,000. Hockey is 17,500 on both, while the Wikipedia list used on this site says 17,367. We print the conflict instead of choosing.

Who plays at T-Mobile Arena?

Home team on the source list: Vegas Golden Knights. League: NHL.
