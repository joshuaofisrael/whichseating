Which seats are best at Barclays Center?

By Which Seating. Last verified 21 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • GA floor. The A to Z guide says it is standing room only, with no seats, a wristband, and entry at the Atlantic Entrance.
  • Five levels. Event level, main concourse, two suite levels, and the upper pavilion.
  • In seat drinks. Sections 7, 8 and 9, and sections 23, 24 and 25, rows 1 to 10. That is a service zone, not a view ranking.
  • Concert chart. The seating page publishes a Concerts and Entertainment file, separate from the Nets and Liberty files, and says charts vary by event.

How to read the chart

Start with the official page linked in the sources. On a basketball seating chart, the lowest seats at midcourt are the closest straight on view of the whole floor. The lowest seats behind a basket look at the near hoop from the baseline. Upper levels are higher. This is our method for reading a chart. It is not a list of section numbers from the venue.

A GA floor ticket is not a seat

On 21 Nov 2026 the Barclays Center A to Z guide says the general admission area on the floor is standing room only and there are no seats. Guests with a GA floor ticket enter at the Atlantic Entrance, get a wristband after they are inside, and are sent to aisles that reach the floor. The guide asks people who want to arrive early to line up no earlier than 9:00 AM, and it says doors open at the same time for everyone. That is a standing product. It is not "row 1 of the floor." If your ticket is a numbered seat, you are not in that paragraph.

The same guide lists five levels: event level, main concourse at street level, Barclays suite level A, suite level B, and the upper pavilion. Suite level guests are pointed at the VIP entrances. Not every concert uses every club. Under club access for concerts, the guide says certain clubs may be open during concerts or as part of a concert package, and it tells you to ask guest services. It does not name the clubs. A club name on a ticket is an amenity question for what club seats include, and this page will not invent the lounge.

The rows that get a drink brought to the seat

In seat beverage service is printed as a location list, not as a view ranking. The A to Z guide puts it in sections 7, 8 and 9, rows 1 to 10, and in sections 23, 24 and 25, rows 1 to 10. You order from a QR code on the armrest, and alcohol still needs a photo ID at delivery. Those six sections are where the arena runs that service. The guide does not say they are the best view of a stage, a court, or a concert screen.

Seat width is a separate printed fact. Seats vary, the guide says, from about 17 inches to about 22 inches. That is the hardware range. It is not a map of which sections are the wide ones.

The concert chart is its own file

The seating charts page says charts may vary by event, and that the accurate chart is the one on the event page. The files it offers by name are Concerts and Entertainment, Brooklyn Nets, and New York Liberty. A Nets ticket and a concert ticket are different downloads. The text around those links does not number the sections behind a stage. If the ticket says limited view or obstructed view, that label is the fact. See sections to skip.

The building is indoor on the arena list, so shade is not the way to choose. Home team on the list: Brooklyn Nets. Guest services centers, for a question the chart does not answer, are on the main concourse across from section 12 and section 112, and on the upper pavilion behind section 212. The NBA hub collects the other indoor arenas.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best seats at Barclays Center?

The arena does not rank them. It separates a Concerts and Entertainment chart from the Nets and Liberty charts, and it says the event page is the accurate one because charts vary.

Is the Barclays Center floor a seat?

Not when the ticket says GA floor. The A to Z guide says that area is standing room only, with no seats. You enter at the Atlantic Entrance and get a wristband.

Which Barclays sections have in seat service?

The A to Z guide lists sections 7, 8 and 9, rows 1 to 10, and sections 23, 24 and 25, rows 1 to 10. It describes drink delivery, not a better view.

Do Barclays clubs open for concerts?

The guide says certain clubs may be open during concerts or as part of a concert package. It does not name which clubs. Ask guest services for that show.

Who plays at Barclays Center?

Home team on the source list: Brooklyn Nets. League: NBA. The seating page also publishes a New York Liberty chart.
