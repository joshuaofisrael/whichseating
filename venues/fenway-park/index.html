Which seats are best at Fenway Park?

By Which Seating. Last verified 20 Nov 2026.

MLBOpenBoston, Massachusetts

Quick answer

  • Close view. MLB.com's 18 March 2026 Fenway guide calls the lower box seats from line to line a close view, and the infield grandstand a strong sightline under a roof.
  • Netting. The Red Sox netting page puts the screen from Field Box Section 79 to Field Box Section 9, about 12 feet 8 inches over the field. It does not call those seats obstructed.
  • Up high in left. Monster Seats sit above the 37 foot wall, installed in 2003. Aura Club seats are behind home plate on the 4th level. The Dell club is season tickets only.
  • Still open air. The stadium list says Open. The grandstand roof covers one band. It is not a dome.

Fenway Park in Boston, Massachusetts is listed as home to Boston Red Sox. Listed capacity 37,755. Roof on the source list: Open.

How to read the chart

Start with the official page linked in the sources. On a baseball seating chart, seats in the lowest level directly behind home plate face the pitcher and the full field. Seats down the first base line or the third base line look across the diamond. Upper levels are farther from home plate. Outfield seats face back toward the infield. This is our method for reading a chart. It is not a list of section numbers from the venue.

What the Red Sox pages actually say

The generic chart method above is still only a method. On 20 Nov 2026 the Red Sox and MLB.com print a handful of Fenway specific facts. Checked 20 Nov 2026. None of them is a ranked list of every section.

Protective netting is the fact that changes the lower bowl. The Red Sox netting page says the screen runs from Field Box Section 79 to Field Box Section 9, with varying height, about 12 feet 8 inches above the field. The page tells fans in those seats to stay alert for bats and batted balls. It does not call the seats obstructed, limited, or partial view. Treat the net as a safety screen in front of a close seat. If the ticket adds an obstructed label, that is a separate fact. See sections to skip.

Ian Browne's MLB.com Fenway guide, dated 18 March 2026, is the sightline writeup the league site is willing to print. It says the park has great sightlines and puts fans on top of the action. Then it splits the bowl into products:

  • Monster Seats, installed above the 37 foot left field wall in 2003. Before that, the guide says, home runs landed in a net or went out to Lansdowne Street.
  • Box seats in the lower bowl from foul line to foul line: a close view, which the guide ties to Fenway's small foul territory.
  • Wooden grandstand seats in the infield: a strong sightline, with a roof overhead that keeps rain off that band.
  • The Sam Deck on the roof in right field: a bar, picnic tables, and a lot of people standing.
  • Bleachers: a looser crowd, and seats near the bullpen with a view of pitchers warming up.

That is five different ways to watch, written by MLB.com, not a Which Seating ranking. The grandstand roof matters for shade and sun because the stadium list still classes Fenway as open air. Open air does not mean every seat is uncovered.

Distances and wall heights

The Red Sox A to Z guide prints the dimensions in feet: 310 down the left field line, 379 in left center, 390 in center, 420 in deep center, 380 in deep right, and 302 down the right field line. The left field wall, the Green Monster, is 37 feet. The center field wall is 17 feet. The bullpen fences are 5 feet. The right field fence is 3 to 5 feet. The 390 feet to center matches the center field figure already in the table below, which came from the Wikipedia stadium list on 16 Nov 2026. The wall heights are why the Monster Seats feel like their own deck: you are looking over a 37 foot wall, not over a ordinary outfield fence.

Fourth level, and the one club sold only by the season

The same A to Z guide describes two premium bands without calling either one the best seat in the park.

The Aura Pavilion Level is the entire 4th and 5th level, including Pavilion seating, Pavilion Box, and the Pavilion suites marked B and K. Pavilion and Aura Club tickets include in seat food and drink, padded seats, and what the page calls unparalleled views of the field and the Boston skyline. Pavilion seating sits along the 4th level on the first and third base lines and can use the Aura Club dining room. Aura Club seating is directly behind home plate on the 4th level, reached through that dining room. Pavilion Box tickets and the B and K suites do not get the dining room. So "4th level" on a Fenway ticket is not one product. Behind the plate and down the lines share a lounge. The box and the named suites do not.

The Dell Technologies Club overlooks the field and the city. Inside, the page says, it is climate controlled, with a restaurant and full service bars. Outside are padded seats with a TV in each section, and the page's own line is that they are some of the best views in baseball. The club is sold as season tickets only. That is a Red Sox claim about the view, and a hard limit on how you buy it. It is not a score we assigned.

Use these names when you read a ticket: Field Box and the net, lower box from line to line, infield grandstand under the roof, Monster Seats, Sam Deck, bleachers, Aura Club behind the plate, Aura Pavilion along the baselines, Dell season seats. If the ticket uses a name this page does not, open the Red Sox page again. Layouts move. How to choose a seat is the order of operations. Frequently asked questions

What are the best seats at Fenway Park?

The Red Sox do not publish one winning section. MLB.com's Fenway guide dated 18 March 2026 calls the lower box seats from line to line a close view and the wooden infield grandstand a strong sightline under a roof. Monster Seats sit above the left field wall. Aura Club seats are directly behind home plate on the 4th level.

Does the Fenway net block the view?

The Red Sox netting page says the screen runs from Field Box Section 79 to Field Box Section 9, about 12 feet 8 inches above the field, and tells fans there to watch for bats and balls. It does not use the words obstructed view.

Which Fenway seats are under a roof?

The MLB.com guide says the wooden grandstand seats in the infield have a roof overhead that keeps rain off. The Dell Technologies Club is climate controlled inside, with padded seats outside. The park is still listed as open air.

What is the difference between the Aura Club and the Pavilion?

Aura Club seats are directly behind home plate on the 4th level and open into the dining room. Pavilion seats run along the 4th level baselines and can use that dining room. Pavilion Box tickets and the B and K suites cannot.

Home team on the source list: Boston Red Sox. League: MLB.
