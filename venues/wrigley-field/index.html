Which seats are best at Wrigley Field?

By Which Seating. Last verified 20 Nov 2026.

MLBOpenChicago, Illinois

Quick answer

  • Labels moved in 2019. Seats did not. The Cubs switched from aisle, row, and seat to section, row, and seat. Season seats stayed put.
  • Upper level. The club says the old 400s, now the 300s, compare with 200 level seats at other parks. The old 500s are now the 400s.
  • Bleachers. Now the 500s. General admission in the regular season, assigned in the postseason, on the FAQ we checked.
  • L and R. Left field and right field, so you can find the ramp. The page's examples are Section 303L and Section 431R.

How to read the chart

Start with the official page linked in the sources. On a baseball seating chart, seats in the lowest level directly behind home plate face the pitcher and the full field. Seats down the first base line or the third base line look across the diamond. Upper levels are farther from home plate. Outfield seats face back toward the infield. This is our method for reading a chart. It is not a list of section numbers from the venue.

Read the section before you trust an old photo

Wrigley tickets changed labels in 2019. The seats did not move. The Cubs renumbering FAQ, still up when we checked it on 20 Nov 2026, is the page that explains the difference.

The old system dated to the early 1980s and used aisle, row, and seat. The Cubs say it left neighboring seats with numbers that did not run in order, and it left upper level seats looking cheaper than the view. Opening Day 2019 switched the park to section, row, and seat. Season ticket holders kept the same physical seat. Only the label changed. An old photo or forum post that cites an aisle number is describing the same chair under a retired name.

The FAQ's own view sentence is specific, and it is theirs, not ours: the old 400 seats are "some of the best in baseball" and "comparable to 200-level seats in other ballparks." To match that, the club renumbered. The FAQ lists three moves. The 400s are now the 300s. The 500s are now the 400s. The Budweiser Bleachers are now the 500s. The bleachers stay general admission in the regular season and become assigned seats in the postseason. That is the whole map the page is willing to print. It does not say which row inside the 300s is the good one.

Upper level sections carry a letter so you can find a ramp. L means left field. R means right field. The examples on the page are Section 303L and Section 431R. The same letters show up on wayfinding signs on the main concourse. Suites kept their old numbers and gained an S in front, for suite level.

The gate page still translates the old letter gates. Marquee Gate was Gate F, at Addison and Clark. Left Field Gate was Gate K, on the Waveland side. The Budweiser Bleacher Gate is at Sheffield and Waveland. If your section ends in L, you are aiming at the left field side of the park, not at a mystery ramp. The letter is a direction, not a quality grade.

What the renumbering does not decide

The FAQ says the upper level was undervalued, and that the old 400s compare with 200 level seats elsewhere. It does not publish a shaded section, an obstructed row, or a price. Bleacher seats in the 500s are a different product from the 300s: general admission on a normal day, assigned if the Cubs are in the postseason, according to that FAQ. Confirm the current chart if you are buying a playoff ticket, because a FAQ can lag a later change. We read it on 20 Nov 2026 and it still said that.

For the wider question of home plate versus a high seat, use where to sit to see the whole field. For a limited view word on the ticket, use sections to skip. This page will not copy a fan site pole list into the gap.

Frequently asked questions

How do I read a Wrigley section number?

Since Opening Day 2019 the Cubs use section, row, and seat. The old aisle numbers described the same chairs. Upper level sections end in L for left field or R for right field. The club's examples are Section 303L and Section 431R.

Are Wrigley's upper seats a bad view?

The Cubs' renumbering FAQ says the old 400 level is some of the best seats in baseball and comparable to 200 level seats at other parks. That level is now the 300s. The old 500s are now the 400s.

Are the Wrigley bleachers general admission?

The same FAQ says the Budweiser Bleachers are now the 500s, stay general admission in the regular season, and switch to assigned seats in the postseason.

Which Wrigley sections are obstructed?

The renumbering FAQ and the gate page do not publish a numbered obstructed list. Skip a seat when the ticket says limited view, obstructed view, or partial view.

Who plays at Wrigley Field?

Home team on the source list: Chicago Cubs. League: MLB.
