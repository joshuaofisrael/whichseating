Which seats are best at United Center?

By Which Seating. Last verified 21 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Two concert files. The arena publishes "Concert with floor seating" and "Concert with general admission floor" as different charts. A Bulls chart and a Blackhawks chart are different again.
  • Club is the 200 level. The club handbook still on the site assumes sections 220 to 232 are obstructed for a 180 degree stage, and sections 221 to 231 for a 270 degree stage. The PDF cites a 2005 smoking ordinance, so treat the ranges as the handbook's assumption.
  • Charts vary. The seating page says the event page is the accurate chart. The text of that page does not list section numbers.
  • Indoor. Sun is not the seating factor. The list classifies the roof as indoor.

How to read the chart

We did not confirm an official seating page on this check, so start with the list source and the venue's own current chart. On a basketball seating chart, the lowest seats at midcourt are the closest straight on view of the whole floor. The lowest seats behind a basket look at the near hoop from the baseline. Upper levels are higher. On a hockey seating chart, the lowest seats at center ice are the closest straight on view of the whole rink. The lowest seats behind a goal look at the near net from the end. Upper levels are higher. This is our method for reading a chart. It is not a list of section numbers from the venue.

Five charts, and two of them are concerts

The generic bowl above is only a method. On 21 Nov 2026 the United Center seating charts page names five separate layouts, and it says they reflect the general layout and may vary by event. The accurate chart, the page says, is the one on that event's page.

What the club handbook still says about a blocked view

Club seats are the 200 level. The club seating handbook still hosted on the arena's site calls them Club Seat (200 level) tickets, and it says guests with 100 or 300 level tickets are on the other bowls. It also says the handbook's concert maps of the 200 level cover several stage shapes. Then it gets specific, and old.

The same PDF cites the City of Chicago Clean Indoor Act of 2005 when it talks about smoking. Treat the section ranges below as the handbook's general assumption for club seats, not as a promise about tonight. The seating charts page, opened the same day, does not repeat these numbers in text.

  • For a 180 degree stage, the handbook says club sections 220 to 232 are assumed to have an obstructed view.
  • For a 270 degree stage, it says club sections 221 to 231 are assumed to have an obstructed view.
  • Obstructed seats, it says, are determined by the performer, event by event. Members in those seats are moved to the next available 200 or 300 level location.
  • For a smaller theatre setup, which the handbook describes as generally 3,000 to 10,000 guests, it says there will be club seats with obstructed views beyond the ones normally restricted, and that draping, lighting, and sound decide which ones.

That is a club rule. It is not a map of sections 101 to 122, and it does not say the 300 level is a bad view of a Bulls game. A game chart and a concert chart answer different questions. Sections to skip is where an obstructed label on a ticket fits. What club seats include is the amenity question, which this handbook answers with a lounge, in seat service, and a 200 level ticket.

Basketball, hockey, and the roof

The building is indoor on the arena list, so sun is not how you pick a seat. The lowest bowl is the 100 level, the club is the 200, and the upper bowl is the 300. For a Bulls game, the closest straight on view of the whole floor is still the lowest seats near midcourt, and the lowest seats behind a basket look at one hoop. That method is ours. The arena's own contribution is the separate chart files, plus the club handbook's concert assumptions. Home teams on the list: Chicago Bulls and Chicago Blackhawks. Frequently asked questions

What are the best seats at the United Center?

The arena does not rank them. It publishes five charts: Blackhawks, Bulls, a concert with floor seating, a concert with a general admission floor, and a premium chart. The event page is the accurate one, and the charts may vary.

Does a United Center concert put seats on the floor?

Sometimes. The arena's seating page has one chart called Concert with floor seating and another called Concert with general admission floor. Read the title of the chart on your event.

Which United Center club seats are obstructed at a concert?

The club handbook still posted by the arena assumes club sections 220 to 232 are obstructed for a 180 degree stage and sections 221 to 231 for a 270 degree stage. The handbook cites a 2005 city ordinance, and the current charts page does not repeat those numbers in text. The performer decides obstructed seats event by event.

Does sun matter at the United Center?

No. The source list classifies the building as indoor.

Who plays at the United Center?

Home teams on the source list: Chicago Bulls and Chicago Blackhawks. Leagues: NBA and NHL.
