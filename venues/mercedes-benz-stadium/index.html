Which seats are best at Mercedes-Benz Stadium?

By Which Seating. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

NFLRetractableAtlanta, Georgia

Quick answer

  • Best view. Start with the official page linked in the sources. On a football seating chart, the closest straight on view of the whole field is the lowest seating level near midfield. The lowest seats in an end zone are close to one end of the field and far from the other. Upper levels sit higher and farther from the field. This is our method for reading a chart. It is not a list of section numbers from the venue.
  • Sun and roof. The source list classifies the roof as retractable. Sun and weather depend on whether the roof is open for that event. The sources do not name shaded sections.
  • Skip. Skip any seat the official page or the ticket marks as limited view, obstructed view, or partial view. The text sources checked for this page did not publish a numbered list of obstructed sections, so none is printed here.
  • Accessible seating. The sources checked for this page do not publish accessible section numbers at Mercedes-Benz Stadium. Use the venue accessibility office, and the accessible path on the official page when one is linked below, before you buy.
  • Value. No value ranking of sections is published in the sources, and this page does not list prices, because prices change by opponent. On a normal chart, upper levels are farther from the playing surface than the lowest level.
