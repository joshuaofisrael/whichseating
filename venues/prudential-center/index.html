Which seats are best at Prudential Center?

By Which Seating. Last verified 21 Nov 2026.

NHLIndoorNewark, New Jersey

Quick answer

  • Two concert maps. The seating page publishes End Stage Seating and Center Stage Seating as separate files, plus Devils and basketball maps.
  • Floor charts vary. The A to Z guide says so. Seat 1 is on the right when you face the floor. Main concourse rows are often 20 to 24 seats. The upper concourse often has 9 to 14 rows.
  • Warm ups are hockey. Sections 1 to 22 are the lower level in a Devils warm up rule. It is not a concert instruction.
  • Concert screens. The house scoreboard is generally not used for the show. The tour brings other screens.

How to read the chart

Start with the official page linked in the sources. On a hockey seating chart, the lowest seats at center ice are the closest straight on view of the whole rink. The lowest seats behind a goal look at the near net from the end. Upper levels are higher. This is our method for reading a chart. It is not a list of section numbers from the venue.

The arena names the stage shape on the map

On 21 Nov 2026 the Prudential Center seating page does not offer one arena map. It offers separate maps, and two of the names are the concert question: End Stage Seating and Center Stage Seating. The same page also lists Devils Seating, Basketball Seating, Luxury Suites, The Lofts, Studio Suites, and M Lounge by BMW. An end stage concert and a center stage concert are different files. A Devils game is a third file. The page says you can look up a Devils seat view. It does not say the Devils view tool works for a concert.

The A to Z guide says the same thing in one sentence under seating information: for concerts and events with floor seating, the seating chart varies. It adds a small reading rule that does travel from event to event. If you sit in a section and face the floor, seat number 1 is on your right. The number of seats in a row, and the number of rows, varies by event. Most main concourse sections have rows of 20 to 24 seats. Upper concourse sections have 9 to 14 rows. Those are typical counts, and the guide says the event can break them.

The lower level rule is a Devils warm up rule

The A to Z guide describes a warm up policy for Devils regular season games. Doors to the end of warm ups, about 20 minutes before puck drop, ticket holders may watch from their own seat. Someone whose ticket is not in the lower level, which the guide defines as sections 1 to 22, may stand in rows 1 to 6 of sections 2, 3, 4, 12, 13, or 14 if that seat is empty. They have to leave when warm ups end. That paragraph is about hockey warm ups. It is not a concert seating recommendation, and it is not permission to move at a show.

The house scoreboard is a concert fact, and it cuts the other way. The guide says the Prudential Center scoreboard is generally not used during concerts. It is used for concert announcements. Other screens are brought by the tour. Buying a seat because it faces the hockey scoreboard does not tell you where the tour's screens will sit.

Levels the guide actually names

The A to Z guide never prints a neat "five levels" list. It names the bowls in passing: main concourse, suite level 1, suite level 2, and the New Jersey Lottery Upper Concourse. Family restrooms and first aid are pinned to sections on those levels, including section 9 and section 114 for guest services. Doors generally open about 60 to 90 minutes before the event, and the guide says the event page can override that. The building is indoor on our list. Home team on the list: New Jersey Devils. The NHL hub is the parent page. How to choose a seat is the order of operations: pick the map whose name matches the ticket, then read the row.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best seats at Prudential Center?

The arena does not rank them. It publishes separate maps for an end stage concert, a center stage concert, Devils hockey, and basketball. The A to Z guide says concert floor charts vary by event.

How do I tell an end stage seat from a center stage seat?

Open the map with that name. End Stage Seating and Center Stage Seating are different links on the arena's seating page.

How many seats are in a Prudential Center row?

It varies by event. The A to Z guide says most main concourse sections have rows of 20 to 24 seats, and upper concourse sections have 9 to 14 rows.

Can I stand in the lower level during warm ups?

At Devils regular season games, the guide allows empty rows 1 to 6 of sections 2, 3, 4, 12, 13, or 14 until warm ups end, if your ticket is not already in sections 1 to 22. That rule is not a concert rule.

Who plays at Prudential Center?

Home team on the source list: New Jersey Devils. League: NHL. The arena also publishes a basketball map and says Seton Hall plays there.
