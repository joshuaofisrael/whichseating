Editorial policy

Last updated 16 Nov 2026. This policy governs Which Seating (whichseating.com), a trade name of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC.

What we publish

Evergreen seat guides. Each venue page answers which seats to pick at that building and which to skip. We do not publish scores, rumors, or ticket prices.

Sourcing

A factual claim needs a source on the page. We prefer the venue's own page and public league or stadium lists. If a detail is not in those sources, we leave it out. We do not invent section numbers, shade maps, or obstructed view lists.

Byline

The byline on a guide is Which Seating, the site. It is not a personal byline. The author in our Article markup is the organization Which Seating.

Dates

datePublished is the first publication date of the page and is not taken from the build clock. dateModified changes only when the page's facts change. The "last verified" line is the date we last checked the sources.

Independence

No team, league, venue, or ticket seller pays for a listing or for a change to a page. We do not display team logos. Names are plain text so you can tell the buildings apart.

Corrections

If a venue publishes a section fact we missed, or a fact on a page is wrong, email joshuaofisrael@gmail.com with the page address and the source. We correct verified mistakes.
