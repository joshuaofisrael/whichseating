How a page is built

We copy capacity, roof, year opened, and home teams from a public list, and we link the official page when a check on 16 Nov 2026 got a response. We do not invent section numbers, shade maps, or obstructed view lists. Sources and methods.

Frequently asked questions

Which seats should I pick?

Open the venue you are attending. The page tells you how to read that chart, and which seats to skip when the ticket says limited view, obstructed view, or partial view. Section numbers appear only when a source prints them.

Which venues does this guide cover?

This guide covers 118 major US stadiums and arenas in the NFL, MLB, NBA, NHL, and college football. A building used by two teams has one page.
